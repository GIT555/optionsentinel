What is "Dynamic Exit"? (Plain English Guide)

TRIGGER 1

50% Max Profit Exit (Locking Gains Early)

When you sell an option for a premium (e.g. sell a Put for $6.00), you do not hold it all the way to expiration for $0.00.

The moment the option price drops to $3.00 (50% profit), the engine signals: BUY TO CLOSE IMMEDIATELY.

Why? You captured 50% of the maximum gain in just 20% to 30% of the total time. Holding longer forces you to risk 100% of your collateral for the remaining tiny gain.
TRIGGER 2

14 DTE Roll / Exit (Gamma Danger Zone)

When an option gets closer than 14 Days to Expiration (DTE), Gamma risk accelerates violently.

A small $1 drop in the stock can cause your option to spike $2 or $3 against you in hours. If the trade hasn't hit 50% profit by 14 DTE, the engine signals: ROLL OUT TO 42 DTE OR CLOSE.

Why? Rolling at 14 DTE closes the near-term volatile contract and sells a fresh 45-day contract further away for another net cash credit.
